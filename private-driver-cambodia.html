Private transport built around your itinerary

Private transport can be useful when you want to travel on your own schedule rather than follow a group departure. Tell Cambodia Transportation where you want to be picked up, where you want to go, your travel date, and whether you need a one-way ride or a return trip.

Services you can request

Airport transfers

Arrange pickup to or from the airport and share your flight details.

Intercity rides

Request transport between Siem Reap, Phnom Penh, Battambang, and other destinations.

City rides

Plan transport around town, hotels, and local destinations.

Private sightseeing

Share the places you want to visit and ask about a custom itinerary.

Choose a vehicle for your group

Private car, SUV, van, and minivan options may be available depending on the route and date. When requesting a quote, include passenger count and luggage so the right vehicle can be recommended. If you need an English-speaking driver, ask for availability when booking.

How to request a ride

Use the Cambodia Transportation website to submit your pickup point, destination, date, time, passenger count, and vehicle preference. Confirm the final fare, inclusions, and pickup arrangements before your journey.

Frequently asked questions

Can I book a private driver for an intercity trip?

You can request an intercity ride by sharing your start point, destination, date, passenger count, and preferred vehicle.

Can I request a return trip?

Include your return date and timing in your request so the availability and fare can be confirmed.

Which vehicle should I choose?

Choose based on the number of passengers and luggage. Ask the team to confirm a suitable option.

Can I create a custom sightseeing trip?

Share your preferred destinations and schedule when requesting a private trip.

Ready to plan your journey?

Visit Cambodia Transportation to request your ride, provide your travel details, and confirm the right vehicle for your journey.
