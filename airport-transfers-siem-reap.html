A smoother arrival in Siem Reap

After a flight, having your pickup arranged can make the first part of your Cambodia trip easier. Cambodia Transportation helps travelers arrange private airport pickups and onward transport in Siem Reap. When requesting a ride, provide your arrival date, flight number, passenger count, luggage needs, and destination.

How to arrange your airport transfer

  1. Open the booking form on Cambodia Transportation.
  2. Choose airport transfer and enter your arrival details.
  3. Provide your flight number, pickup date and time, destination, and preferred vehicle.
  4. Confirm the quoted price and pickup instructions before travelling.

Choose the right vehicle

Tell us how many people are travelling and how much luggage you have. This helps match your request to a suitable private car, SUV, van, or minivan. If you need an English-speaking driver, mention that when booking so availability can be confirmed.

Explore more of Cambodia

Siem Reap can be the starting point for temple visits and longer journeys. You can also ask about intercity rides to Phnom Penh, Battambang, and other destinations. Long-distance fares may depend on the route and requested vehicle.

Frequently asked questions

What details should I provide for an airport pickup?

Provide your arrival date, flight number, arrival time, number of passengers, luggage needs, and destination.

Can I request a transfer to my hotel?

Yes. Enter your hotel or accommodation as the destination when requesting your ride.

Can I request an English-speaking driver?

You can request an English-speaking driver when booking. Confirm availability before your trip.

How do I confirm the price?

Submit your route and vehicle preference, then confirm the quoted price with Cambodia Transportation before travel.

Ready to plan your journey?

Visit Cambodia Transportation to request your ride, provide your travel details, and confirm the right vehicle for your journey.
